---
**FounderHive** — call a teammate with a slash command, e.g. `/honey run the weekly review` or
`/max challenge my pricing`. Each drafts for your approval and never acts externally.

- `/honey` — Chief of Staff: priorities, weekly review, **organise my files**
- `/jason` — GTM: prospecting, outreach, market signals, weekly GTM brief
- `/dawn` — Content: blogs & LinkedIn in your voice
- `/tarik` — Customer Success: meeting briefs, follow-ups, partner tracking
- `/deap` — Product: PRDs, user stories, roadmap
- `/simon` — Investor Relations: one-pager, deck, data room, funding packs
- `/james` — CTO: architecture, feasibility, deployment readiness
- `/nora` — People / Legal / Finance: hiring, contracts, expenses, runway (prep only)
- `/max` — Co-Founder: stress-tests your idea (market, pricing, competition, GTM, tech, AI governance, ethics)

First time? Run the **setup** agent to configure `config.yaml` + your company context. $ARGUMENTS
