# Messaging — Brightbook (example)

## Core message
**Tidy books for freelancers — in minutes a month.** Brightbook sorts your expenses, chases your invoices,
and gets you tax-ready, so you can get back to the work you actually love.

## Value props (by worry)
- "I dread the tax deadline" → Brightbook keeps you ready all year; no January floor-and-shoebox.
- "I never chase invoices" → it chases them politely, for you.
- "I don't know what I can expense" → it sorts and nudges, and flags what to ask your accountant.
- "Accounting software is overkill" → built only for freelancers; nothing you don't need.

## Differentiation
Freelancer-first + genuinely simple + AI does the boring bits + £9/mo. Not small-business software.

## Do / don't
- DO lead with the feeling and the "minutes a month" promise. DON'T use finance jargon or imply it replaces
  an accountant or gives tax advice.
