# ICP — Brightbook (example)

- **Who pays:** solo freelancers / sole traders earning enough to care about tax (~£15k+), who find admin
  stressful and don't want a bookkeeper. Buyer = user.
- **Hot segments:** creatives (designers, writers, photographers), independent developers/contractors, and
  new freelancers in their first tax year (highest anxiety → highest pull).
- **Why now:** a January tax deadline, a first invoice that didn't get paid, or a scary "set aside for tax"
  moment. Life events, not quarters.
- **Not for:** small businesses with employees or an existing bookkeeper (that's FreeAgent/QuickBooks land).

## Channels / wedges
Freelancer communities & Slacks · SEO ("freelancer tax uk", "how much to set aside for tax") · creator
partnerships · accountant referral partnerships (Brightbook makes *their* job easier).
