# Outreach Voice — Brightbook (example)

Brightbook's growth is mostly self-serve + content, but for **partnerships** (accountants, freelancer
communities, creator newsletters) outreach is warm, short, human — Sam's voice (see `founder-voice.md`).

## Principles
- 4–6 sentences. Lead with *their* audience's pain, not Brightbook's features.
- One concrete offer (a free partner plan, a co-written guide, a revenue share). Soft CTA.
- No jargon, no hype. British spelling.

## Example (accountant partnership)
> Hi [name], I run Brightbook — a dead-simple bookkeeping app for freelancers. Most of our users have an
> accountant (you), and we make their year-end pack tidy and ready, which saves you the back-and-forth. I'd
> love to set you up with a free partner account and see if it saves your freelance clients (and you) some
> admin. Worth a quick chat?

## Hard rule
Drafts only — never send to anyone but the founder for approval.
