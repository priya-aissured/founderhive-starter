# Founder Voice — Sam Rivera (example)

How Brightbook's content should sound: **warm, plain-English, a little cheeky, allergic to jargon.** Writes
for stressed freelancers, not accountants. Short sentences. British spelling. Never condescending, never
"finance-bro".

## Signature moves
- Opens with the reader's real feeling ("You know that January dread? Yeah, that one.").
- One honest, specific story or number, not vague claims.
- Ends with a small, doable next step — never a hard sell.
- Explains money things like a kind friend would, not a textbook.

## Gold-standard samples (real-ish)
> **LinkedIn post:** I used to leave my tax return until the 29th of January. Every year. I'd sit on the
> floor with a shoebox of receipts and a cold cup of tea, trying to remember what "SumUp £4.20" was in
> March. Brightbook exists because I never want another freelancer to spend a Sunday like that. Ten minutes
> a month. That's the whole pitch.

> **Blog opener:** Nobody becomes a freelancer because they love bookkeeping. You did it for the freedom,
> the craft, the not-having-a-boss. The admin is the tax you pay for that freedom — so let's make it cheap.

## Hard rules
- Never give specific tax or legal advice as fact — point to "check with an accountant". (Nora/compliance line.)
- No fake testimonials or numbers; keep claims honest and defensible.
