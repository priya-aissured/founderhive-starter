# Company Overview — Brightbook (example)

## Brightbook
- **One-liner:** Tidy books for freelancers — sort expenses, chase invoices, get tax-ready in minutes a month.
- **What it does:** connects to your bank (Open Banking), auto-categorises income & expenses, sends and
  chases invoices, estimates your tax set-aside, and produces a tidy year-end pack for you or your accountant.
- **Who it's for:** solo freelancers, contractors and sole traders — not small businesses with a bookkeeper.
  The buyer *is* the user (prosumer, self-serve).
- **The pain:** freelancers dread admin and the tax deadline; existing tools are built for small businesses
  and feel heavy; spreadsheets fall apart; accountants are overkill for a solo earner.
- **Differentiation:** radically simple, freelancer-first, "minutes a month"; AI does the boring sorting
  and chasing. Not trying to be full accounting software.

## Market (example figures — illustrative)
- ~4.3M self-employed in the UK; tens of millions across the US/EU. Growing creator/freelance economy.
- Adjacent spend: freelancers already pay for design tools, a domain, etc. — a £9/mo habit is normal.

## Pricing (current)
- **Free** (track income/expenses) · **Pro £9/mo** (invoicing, chasing, tax estimates, year-end pack).

## Competition
- FreeAgent, QuickBooks Self-Employed, Coconut-style apps · spreadsheets · a human accountant · "do nothing".
- Edge vs all: built *only* for the solo freelancer, dead simple, AI-assisted, cheap.

## Traction (example)
- ~1,200 active users; ~£6k MRR; 4.7★ app rating; strong word-of-mouth in freelancer communities.

## Honesty / scope
- Brightbook is a bookkeeping *aid*, not an accountant. It never files taxes or gives binding tax advice —
  it prepares and points users to a professional where needed.
