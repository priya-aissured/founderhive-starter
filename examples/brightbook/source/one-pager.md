# Brightbook — one-pager (example source doc)

**Tidy books for freelancers — in minutes a month.**

**Problem.** Freelancers didn't sign up to do bookkeeping. Receipts pile up, invoices go unchased, and the
January tax deadline is a yearly panic. Existing tools (FreeAgent, QuickBooks) are built for small
businesses with a bookkeeper; spreadsheets break; an accountant is overkill for a solo earner.

**Product.** Brightbook connects to your bank via Open Banking, auto-categorises income and expenses, sends
and politely chases invoices, estimates how much to set aside for tax, and produces a tidy year-end pack for
you or your accountant. Designed to take minutes a month.

**Market.** ~4.3M UK self-employed; tens of millions across the US/EU; a growing freelance/creator economy.
Freelancers already pay for tools — a £9/mo habit is normal.

**Model.** Freemium: Free (tracking) → Pro £9/mo (invoicing, chasing, tax estimates, year-end pack).

**Traction (illustrative).** ~1,200 active users, ~£6k MRR, 4.7★, strong word-of-mouth in freelancer
communities.

**Why we win.** Freelancer-first and radically simple; AI does the boring sorting and chasing; a tenth the
complexity of small-business accounting software, at a price a freelancer doesn't think twice about.

**Not.** Brightbook is a bookkeeping aid, not an accountant. It doesn't file taxes or give binding tax advice.
