# Brightbook — roadmap (example source doc)

## Now (live)
Open Banking bank feeds · AI expense/income categorisation · invoicing + polite auto-chasing · tax
set-aside estimate · year-end accountant pack · iOS + web.

## Next (0–3 months)
- Android app · receipt capture (photo → matched to transaction) · smarter categorisation (learns per user)
- "Am I ready for tax?" live checklist · export to the top accountant tools.

## Later (3–9 months)
- VAT support (unlock the contractor segment) · mileage tracking · multi-currency for cross-border freelancers
- Accountant partner portal (partners manage several freelance clients).

## Tech notes
Open Banking aggregator dependency (single point of failure — watch it); categorisation model accuracy is
the core quality metric; financial data = strict privacy + encryption.

## Honest gaps
No VAT yet (blocks contractors); single bank aggregator; categorisation still needs user corrections.
