# Brightbook — pricing & unit economics (example source doc)

## Plans
- **Free** — connect one bank account, track income/expenses, basic categories.
- **Pro — £9/mo (or £90/yr)** — unlimited accounts, invoicing + auto-chasing, tax set-aside estimate,
  year-end accountant pack, priority support.

## Rationale
- £9 sits below the "think about it" threshold for a working freelancer and roughly matches one design-tool
  subscription. Annual plan improves retention + cash.
- Free tier is the top of the funnel (tracking is the gateway drug); invoicing + tax features are the
  willingness-to-pay moment (they appear exactly when the pain spikes).

## Illustrative economics
- Blended ARPU ≈ £7/mo (free→paid conversion ~18%). CAC target < £25 via content/communities/partnerships.
- Payback target < 6 months; gross margin high (software + bank-feed costs are small per user).
- Biggest lever: **retention** — freelancers churn when work dries up, so annual plans + "always ready for
  tax" stickiness matter more than top-of-funnel.

## Open questions (for the Co-Founder to poke)
- Is £9 leaving money on the table for VAT-registered contractors who'd pay £19?
- Does free cannibalise paid, or feed it?
- Can CAC stay < £25 beyond the early community, or does paid acquisition break the model?
